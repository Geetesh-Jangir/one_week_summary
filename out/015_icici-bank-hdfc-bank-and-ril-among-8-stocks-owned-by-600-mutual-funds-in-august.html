    ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, and RIL among 8 stocks owned by 600 mutual funds in August

    Written by
    Surbhi Khanna
    , ET Online|
    Sep 16, 2026, 03:29:49 PM IST
    1/7

    Most-owned stocks by mutual funds

    Favourite stocks are those based on the maximum holding count by mutual fund schemes for each stock. Here are the top eight stocks owned by over 600 mutual fund schemes in August, according to data by ACE MF.

    Getty Images
    2/7

    Three banking stocks

    ICICI Bank was held by 775 funds and mutual funds had 208 crore shares of this stock worth Rs 3.03 lakh crore. HDFC Bank was held by 739 funds and mutual funds had 402 crore shares worth Rs 2.85 lakh crore. SBI was held by 701 funds and mutual funds had 126 crore shares worth Rs 1.33 lakh crore.

    Agencies
    3/7

    Bharti Airtel

    The stock was held by 694 mutual fund schemes. The total market value was Rs 1.38 lakh crore, and mutual funds had 76 crore shares of Bharti Airtel.

    Agencies
    4/7

    ​Reliance Industries

    The stock was held by 662 mutual fund schemes. The total market value was Rs 1.71 lakh crore, and mutual funds held 134 crore shares of RIL.

    ETMarkets.com
    5/7

    Axis Bank

    The stock was held by 656 mutual fund schemes. The total market value was Rs 1.40 crore, and mutual funds held 107 crore shares of Axis Bank.

    ANI
    6/7

    Infosys

    Infosys was held by 611 funds. Mutual funds held 86.43 crore shares of Infosys in their portfolio worth Rs 97,928 crore.

    L&T
