GE Vernova T&D India Publishes Postal Ballot Advertisement for USD 151 Million RPT

Key Highlights

GE Vernova T&D India Limited has published newspaper advertisements in Financial Express and Jansatta regarding the dispatch of postal ballot notice seeking shareholder approval for material related party transactions worth up to USD 151 million with GE Grid Solutions LLC. The e-voting process is scheduled from April 17 to May 16, 2026, with results to be announced by May 19, 2026.

GE Vernova T&D India Limited has issued a postal ballot notice seeking shareholder approval for material related party transactions with GE Grid Solutions LLC, highlighting the company's strategic business relationships within the GE Vernova group. The company has now published newspaper advertisements regarding the dispatch of the postal ballot notice as per regulatory requirements.

Transaction Details and Approval Requirements

The company is seeking approval for transactions involving sale and purchase of goods and rendering of services with GE Grid Solutions LLC, a fellow subsidiary within the GE Vernova group. The proposed transaction details are outlined below:

Parameter: Details
Transaction Value: Up to USD 151 million
Transaction Type: Sale and purchase of goods and/or availing and rendering of services
Resolution Type: Ordinary Resolution
Approval Threshold: 31.7% of annual consolidated turnover

Under SEBI regulations, the proposed transactions require shareholder approval as they exceed 10% of the company's annual consolidated turnover. The transactions are expected to be conducted at arm's length basis, in the ordinary course of business, and in the interest of the company.

Newspaper Advertisement Publication

Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the company has published newspaper advertisements in Financial Express (all editions) and Jansatta (New Delhi) regarding the dispatch of the postal ballot notice. The advertisements provide details about the postal ballot process and related information for shareholders.

Publication Details: Information
English Daily: Financial Express (all editions)
Hindi Daily: Jansatta (New Delhi)
Publication Date: April 17, 2026
Regulation: SEBI LODR Regulation 30

E-Voting Schedule and Process

The company has established a comprehensive timeline for the postal ballot process, with all voting to be conducted through remote e-voting only:

Timeline Element: Date/Period
Cut-off Date: Friday, April 10, 2026
E-voting Commencement: Friday, April 17, 2026, 9:00 A.M. (IST)
E-voting Conclusion: Saturday, May 16, 2026, 5:00 P.M. (IST)
Result Announcement: On or before Tuesday, May 19, 2026

Members whose names appear in the Register of Members or Register of Beneficial Owners as on the cut-off date will be eligible to participate in the voting process. The company has appointed National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) to facilitate the electronic voting process.

Related Party Transaction Background

GE Grid Solutions LLC, based in the United States, is part of the Electrification segment of the GE Vernova group. The entity enables power utilities and industries to effectively manage electricity from generation to consumption, helping improve grid reliability, efficiency, and stability. Both companies are fellow subsidiaries of GE Vernova Inc., establishing their related party status.

Historical Transaction Data

The explanatory statement provides context on previous transactions between the entities:

Period: Transaction Value
Financial Year 2024-25: INR 702.0 million
April 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025: INR 963.6 million
Proposed Transaction: Up to USD 151 million (approx. INR 13,600 million)

Business Rationale and Strategic Benefits

The company has outlined several strategic justifications for the proposed transactions. For sales activities, the transactions are expected to lead to increased orders, higher sales volumes, and improved contribution margins through better pricing. These factors are anticipated to enhance overall net profitability and strengthen the company's competitive position while providing access to international markets.

For procurement activities, the company sources specific products and components from GE Grid Solutions LLC due to its technical expertise and specialized manufacturing capabilities. These components are integral to the composite products that the company manufactures and are specifically associated with the proprietary GE Vernova brand and technology.

Regulatory Compliance and Governance

The Audit Committee and Board of Directors approved and recommended the proposed transactions at their respective meetings held on March 18, 2026. The company's transfer pricing policy, based on OECD guidelines and Income Tax Act provisions, ensures that all transactions are conducted at arm's length basis. An independent transfer pricing consultant benchmarks the pricing principles to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

The postal ballot notice has been distributed electronically to eligible shareholders, and the results will be announced within two working days from the last date of remote e-voting. Mr. Manish Gupta of M/s. RMG & Associates, Company Secretaries, has been appointed as the scrutinizer to ensure a fair and transparent voting process.

Market Significance

Block trades represent large-volume transactions typically executed by institutional investors, mutual funds, or other significant market participants. These transactions are conducted outside the regular market to minimize price impact and ensure efficient execution of large orders.

The substantial transaction value of Rs. 39.91 crores indicates meaningful institutional activity in GE Vernova T&D India Ltd.'s equity. Such trades often reflect strategic portfolio decisions, stake adjustments, or investment rebalancing by large financial institutions.

Transaction Analysis
